### 1 · Fabrication Works

**Slug:** `fabrication`
**Summary:** Structural fabrication of industrial components — girders, I-beams, C-channels, plates, storage tanks, and prefabricated shed construction for industrial facilities.
**Full description:**
In order to aid and quarter the process system, structural fabrication is compulsory with different shapes and sizes of sectioned steels such as Girders, Angles, I-beams, C-channels, Plates etc. We not only fabricate individual parts and sections of the project but also supply complete factory outbuildings including those to house machinery.

**Key deliverables:**
- Structural fabrication (girders, angles, I-beams, C-channels, plates)
- Pre-fabricated shed construction (rafters, box columns, purlins)
- Heavy crane support artefacts and platforms
- Machinery substructure and heavy-duty support frameworks
- Storage tanks and silo fabrication
- Sectors served: minerals & ore processing, sugar plants, cement plants, power plants

### 2 · Mechanical Works

**Slug:** `mechanical`
**Summary:** Heating, cooling, refrigeration, piping and plumbing systems — installation, production, and ongoing maintenance and repair for industrial and commercial buildings.
**Full description:**
Merab Engineering Services Limited is in charge of heating or cooling systems, refrigerators, piping and plumbing. We have an extensive range of expertise that, when put together, comprises a huge part of creating and maintaining a building so that it fulfils its purpose. Anything with mechanical parts will require the attention of Merab Engineering Services Limited whether for production, installation or ongoing maintenance and repair.

Temperature control in a building is a large part of our work, especially because heating systems or air conditioners are required in new buildings to keep the temperature at a comfortable level. We look at the building and determine the best method needed for heating and cooling, taking into consideration practical installation costs and energy efficiency.

Our responsibilities include making sure mechanical elements of the building are installed on time and within budget, scheduling of subcontractors, and allocating and delegating of workloads.

**Key deliverables:**
- HVAC — heating, ventilation, air conditioning
- Refrigeration systems
- Piping and plumbing installation
- Maintenance and repair of mechanical systems
- Budget and schedule management for mechanical packages

### 6 · Labour Supply & Scaffolding

**Slug:** `labour-scaffolding`
**Summary:** Supply of skilled and unskilled labour — welders, fitters, fabricators, pipe-fitters — and scaffolding services for industrial, refinery, oil & gas, and construction sites.
**Full description:**
We are into general contracts such as labour supply — skilled and unskilled — trained professionals in welding, fittings, fabrication, pipeline jobs, refinery, oil and gas flow stations, building constructions, industrial elevated access, safety installations, structural steel works, repairs and replacement parts, etc.

We have skilled workers in various fields that are well trained in safety and are of good behaviour. Merab Engineering Services Limited have worked with and for many companies in this aspect and have shown competency in supply of labour and human resources management. We have safety kits, a well-equipped retainership hospital, and also make available working tools and machines.

All employees are trained to live a safety lifestyle every day, both on and off facility. Security personnel will be deployed for the safe keeping of both company materials and personnel.

**Key deliverables:**
- Skilled labour: welders, fitters, fabricators, pipe-fitters
- Unskilled labour supply
- Scaffolding erection and dismantling for industrial access
- Safety training and PPE provision
- Retainership hospital / welfare support
- Human resources management
